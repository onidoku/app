read like a demon.

you follow a webcomic on its own site, a web novel somewhere else, and your own collection on a Komga or Kavita server. that's three tabs, three bookmark folders and no idea which one updated today. onidoku pulls them into one place.

✦ a reader that gets out of the way
• distraction-free reading for comics and novels: no sidebars, pop-ups or autoplaying ads.
• comics your way: long-strip scrolling or page by page, fit to width or height.
• novels your way: light, dark and sepia themes, with the fonts and spacing you like.
• keyboard-first: N / P for chapters, arrow keys for pages, C for the chapter list, ? for the rest.
• works on almost any chapter page, even sites onidoku has never seen: it finds the images or text on its own.
• read without the site: open a chapter on onidoku's own page, with none of the site's scripts or ads.

✦ one library for everything
• add any series in one click from the toolbar, and pick up exactly where you left off.
• search and explore across every source you've added: popular lists, latest updates, all in one place.
• bring your own server: browse and read your Komga and Kavita libraries (comics, CBZ/CBR, PDF and EPUB), with read progress synced back to the server.

✦ never miss a chapter
• background checks for new chapters across your whole library.
• notifications and a badge count the moment something new drops.

✦ any site, your choice
onidoku ships with no sources installed, and you decide which sites it works with:
• install a suggested source for public-domain and openly licensed sites like Standard Ebooks and Pepper&Carrot,
• or write your own in minutes: a source is a small JSON file of CSS selectors, no code.

nothing is hosted, mirrored or unlocked: onidoku shows what the site already shows you, the way it should have looked.

✦ yours, and only yours
• no account, no ads, no tracking. nothing is sent to us.
• your library, progress and settings stay in your browser, with one-click backup and restore.
• source available: https://github.com/onidoku/app
