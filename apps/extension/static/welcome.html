welcome to onidoku

reader mode, a library and new-chapter alerts for the comic and novel sites you already read. everything stays in your browser.

  • reader moderead chapters in a clean reader, without the site’s ads and clutter.
  • libraryfollow series and pick up where you left off.
  • new chaptersonidoku checks your series and tells you when something new is out.
  • search and explorefind series across every source you’ve added, in one place.

set up

  1. offer the reader on any site onidoku spots chapter pages and offers reader mode. this needs access to all sites; pages are checked on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. without it, the reader is still one click away in the toolbar popup.
    ✓ on
  2. free sources to start with already installed: works that are in the public domain or openly licensed for sharing. allow access to their sites so onidoku can read them and check for new chapters.
    ✓ ready
    • connect Komga or Kavita if you run your own media server, read its comics, manga and books here, with progress synced both ways. no server? skip this.

    • use your Paperback repos onidoku runs sources from Paperback 0.8 extension repos. they’re code written by the repo’s authors, so each runs in its own sandbox, and needs access to all sites to load pages and images.
